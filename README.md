**🎗️ Before It's Too Late — Pink October**

----------------------------------------------------
**Before It's Too Late**

is an interactive educational visual novel developed for Pink October (*Octobre Rose*), a breast cancer awareness campaign at the University Akli Mouhand Oulhadj in Bouira, Algeria.

The project raises awareness about breast cancer, the importance of early detection, and the impact of fear, social taboos, and delayed medical consultation. Through an emotional, choice-driven story, players follow two university students, Amira and Nour, whose different decisions highlight the importance of seeking medical advice as early as possible.

----------------------------------------------------
✨ **Key Features**

- **Interactive storytelling:** Make decisions that influence the story and lead to three possible endings.
- **Breast cancer awareness:** Learn about warning signs, risk awareness, and the importance of early medical consultation.
- **Educational quiz:** Answer true-or-false questions and earn awareness points.
- **Self-examination guide:** Explore the steps of breast self-awareness and learn when to consult a healthcare professional.
- **Multilingual experience:** Available in French, English, and Arabic.
- **Animated visuals:** Custom SVG characters, animated backgrounds, transitions, and floating pink petals.
- **Interactive interface:** Dialogue history, sound controls, fullscreen mode, and language selection.
- **Responsive design:** An interface designed to adapt to different screen sizes.

----------------------------------------------------

🛠️ **Technologies**

- HTML5
- CSS3 — animations, responsive layouts, and visual effects
- Vanilla JavaScript — story progression, interactive choices, quizzes, and language management
- SVG — character illustrations and visual elements
- Web Audio API — audio functionality


----------------------------------------------------


🚀 **Getting Started**

No installation or build process is required.

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern web browser.
3. Click **Start** to begin the experience.
4. Make your choices, explore the story, and complete the awareness quiz.


----------------------------------------------------

🎯 **Project Goal**

The goal is to make breast cancer awareness more accessible and engaging for university students by combining storytelling, interactive learning, and emotional engagement.
